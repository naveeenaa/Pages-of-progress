Common short forms Small words stand for letters: “sailor” is AB or TAR, “right” is R, “left” is L, “good” is G, “quiet” is P, “love” is O. Directions: “north” is N, “south” is S, “east” is E, “west” is W.

How to solve Count the letters. Find the definition (check both ends). Look for the indicator word to see what type of clue it is. Work out the wordplay. Check that the answer fits both parts. Fill it in and use the crossing letters to help with the others.
